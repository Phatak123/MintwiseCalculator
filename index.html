What these estimates can—and can’t—tell you.

Calculators are useful for exploring “what if” questions. They are only as accurate as their inputs and assumptions.

General information only. MoneyMap provides educational estimates, not financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Consider your circumstances and consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

How EMI is estimated

For a fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is calculated using the principal, monthly interest rate, and number of monthly payments. A longer term usually lowers the monthly amount but can increase total interest.

Compounding over time

Compound growth means returns are calculated on both the original amount and prior returns. Our estimate assumes one steady return, while real markets rise and fall.

Build a useful budget

Start with take-home income, then list recurring essentials, flexible costs, and savings or debt goals. Include annual and irregular expenses by setting aside a monthly amount.

Check your assumptions

Try a range of interest rates, time periods, and contribution amounts. Compare these estimates with actual lender terms, account fees, taxes, and local rules.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators free?

Yes. They run in your browser and do not require an account. MoneyMap does not send the values you enter to a server.

Are the results financial advice?

No. Results are general estimates for learning and planning. They do not consider your full financial situation or recommend a particular product.

Why may a lender’s EMI differ?

Lenders may include fees, insurance, different compounding methods, variable rates, payment dates, or rounding rules that are not part of this estimate.

Does the growth estimate guarantee returns?

No. The rate is an assumption only. Investment values can fluctuate, and you may receive less than you invested. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
